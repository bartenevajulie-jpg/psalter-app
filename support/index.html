Psalter — Support

Psalter (Псалтирь · Salterio · Psautier) is a Christian app for reading one psalm a day, with Orthodox and Catholic public-domain translations.

Contact

Questions, bug reports or suggestions: open an issue on GitHub. We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked

Why is Psalm 22 the "Lord is my shepherd"? Psalms follow the Septuagint/Vulgate numbering used by Orthodox and traditional Catholic Psalters; the Hebrew number is shown in brackets.

How do I add the widget? Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and choose Psalter.

How do I set the verse as wallpaper? Tap the share button on the Today screen → Save Image, then choose it in Settings → Wallpaper.
